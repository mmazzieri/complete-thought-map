# complete-thought-map

Software implementing the notebook system of Lion Kimbro's book
*How to Make a Complete Map of Every Thought You Think* (2003).

Version 1.0 will be the "hybrid" program exactly as the book describes it in
chapter VI, *The Question of Computers*: you keep your thoughts on paper during
the day, transcribe the pan-subject speeds at night, and the program maintains
your speed lists and tables of contents and prints the updated pages for your
binders. Work in progress.
